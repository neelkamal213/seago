The Story So Far

G. Gopish Kumar founded Seagö to bring zero-emission propulsion to India's rivers, backwaters, and coastlines — motors, battery packs, and boats engineered specifically for the way Indian fishing fleets, tourism operators, and public agencies actually use the water.

Under his leadership, Seagö has grown from an idea into a working fleet in just over two years: manufacturing out of Chennai, selling and supporting customers from Kerala, and now active across 6 states. He has driven the company's partnerships with research and government institutions — including ICAR-CIFT Mumbai on India's first electric fishing vessel, and IIT Madras and the Indian Maritime University on early-stage hydrogen fuel-cell research — bringing new propulsion technology from the lab to the water.
